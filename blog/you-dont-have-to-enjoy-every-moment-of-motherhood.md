One of the most common pieces of advice new mothers hear is:

*"Enjoy every moment. They grow up so fast."*

And while it's true that childhood passes quickly, this advice can sometimes leave mothers feeling guilty when they aren't enjoying every moment.

Because the truth is...

Some moments are beautiful.

And some moments are simply hard.

The midnight wake-ups.

The endless laundry.

The meals that go untouched.

The days when you've been touched, needed, and called for so many times that you can barely hear your own thoughts.

Loving your child and struggling with motherhood can exist at the same time.

One does not cancel out the other.

## The Difference Between Loving Motherhood and Loving Your Child

Many mothers quietly worry:

*"If I'm not enjoying this, does that mean I'm doing something wrong?"*

Not at all.

You can adore your child and still find certain stages exhausting.

You can be grateful and tired.

Present and overwhelmed.

Deeply in love and desperately in need of a break.

These feelings are not contradictions.

They are part of being human.

## The Moments We Don't Talk About

Social media often shows motherhood at its sweetest:

The cuddles.

The smiles.

The matching outfits.

The first steps.

But motherhood is also:

Holding a crying baby while crying yourself.

Eating cold meals.

Feeling lonely despite never being alone.

Wondering if you're doing enough.

Missing parts of your old life.

These moments deserve compassion too.

## What New Mothers Need Most

Not more pressure.

Not more perfection.

Not more comparisons.

What most new mothers need is permission.

Permission to rest.

Permission to ask for help.

Permission to make mistakes.

Permission to have difficult days.

Permission to be a whole person—not just a mother.

## A Gentle Reminder

One day, you may miss this season.

But that doesn't mean you have to enjoy every minute of it while you're living it.

Some days are meant to be cherished.

Some days are simply meant to be survived.

Both are part of the story.

And if today feels heavy, know this:

You do not have to enjoy every moment to be a wonderful mother.

You only have to keep showing up with love.

And chances are, you're already doing that far better than you think.

💛

If you're a new mother reading this, let this be your reminder today:

It's okay to love your child deeply and still have hard days.
