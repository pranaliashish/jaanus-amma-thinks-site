Some of the most beautiful art doesn't come from a craft store.

Sometimes it comes from a walk outside.

A tiny leaf.

A feather.

A flower petal.

A blade of grass.

With just a little imagination, these simple treasures can become a beautiful gallery that catches the sunlight and reminds your toddler of their outdoor adventures.

---



## What You'll Need

- A sunny window or glass door
- Clear contact paper or painter's tape
- Nature treasures like leaves, petals, feathers, or small flowers

Choose only items that have already fallen naturally, and avoid picking flowers or leaves from living plants.

---



## How to Play

After a nature walk, invite your toddler to look at all the little treasures they collected.

Tape a sheet of clear contact paper (sticky side out) or use small pieces of painter's tape on a window.

Then let your little one gently stick each treasure onto the glass.

They can arrange them however they like.

There is no right or wrong design.

Some children create patterns.

Others simply enjoy placing one leaf next to another.

When the sunlight shines through, the window becomes a beautiful piece of nature-inspired art.

---



## Why Toddlers Love It

Toddlers love creating with real objects.

They enjoy touching different textures, choosing where things belong, and seeing their artwork displayed where everyone can admire it.

The changing sunlight makes the activity feel magical throughout the day.

---



## What Your Toddler Is Learning

While decorating the window, they're also developing:

**Fine Motor Skills**

Picking up and placing small objects strengthens little fingers.

**Creativity**

Children experiment with colors, shapes, and patterns.

**Observation Skills**

They begin noticing how leaves, petals, and feathers are different from one another.

**Appreciation for Nature**

Simple treasures become something worth noticing and caring for.

---



## Easy Variations

Want to make it even more fun?

Try creating:

🍂 An autumn window

🌸 A flower rainbow

🪶 A feather collage

🌿 A nature alphabet using leaves shaped like different letters

Or simply let your child decide the theme.

Their imagination often creates the best masterpieces.
