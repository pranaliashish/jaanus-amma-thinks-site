Somewhere high above the sleeping world, there lived a little moon.

Every night, it floated quietly across the sky, watching the glowing villages below, the sleepy rivers, and the tiny lanterns shining in windows.

The stars always seemed calm and certain of themselves.

They sparkled in the same way every night.

But the little moon was different.

Every few nights… *it changed*.

Sometimes it became round and bright, lighting up the whole sky like a silver lantern.

Sometimes it became smaller.

Sometimes half.

Sometimes only a tiny silver curve hidden between clouds.

And the little moon did not understand why.

At first, the moon tried not to think about it.

But little by little, the changing began to hurt its heart.

The stars always looked complete.

The little moon never stayed the same long enough to feel certain of itself.

One quiet night, the moon whispered softly to the sky:

“*The stars never disappear… But sometimes… I almost do.*”

The clouds drifted slowly around it.

The world below slept peacefully.

But the little moon stayed awake, full of worry.

Whenever the moon became smaller, it tried to hide behind clouds.

It did not want the sky to see it fading.

It thought:

“*What if people only love me when I am full and bright?*”

So during its smallest nights, the moon stayed quiet and hidden, afraid that nobody would notice it anymore.

Afraid that becoming smaller made it less beautiful.

Less important.

Less lovable.

Then one night, the moon became only a tiny crescent.

Small. Soft. Barely glowing.

The little moon felt certain nobody would look for it now.

But down below…something surprising happened.

Children still searched the sky for it.

Some pointed excitedly when they found its tiny silver glow peeking through the clouds.

Some smiled quietly.

Some simply felt comforted knowing the moon was still there.

Even in its smallest form.

Even when it was not full.

The little moon watched silently.

Confused.

Gentle warmth slowly spread through its heart.

Then the stars whispered softly around it:

“*You are still the moon… even when only a small part of you shines.*”

The little moon grew very still.

For the first time, it understood something beautiful.

**It did not have to stay full forever to be loved.**

It did not have to shine brightly every moment to matter.

Its changing shape did not make it less worthy.

It simply made it real.

After that night, the little moon stopped hiding behind clouds.

When it was full, it glowed joyfully.

When it became small, it rested peacefully.

And every night, no matter its shape, the moon remembered:

It was still the moon. Still loved. Still enough.

Gentle Reflection 🌙

Sometimes we change.

Sometimes we feel bright and confident.

Sometimes we feel quiet, tired, emotional, or small.

But we are still worthy of love in every phase of who we are.

**Even the moon does not stay full forever. ✨**
