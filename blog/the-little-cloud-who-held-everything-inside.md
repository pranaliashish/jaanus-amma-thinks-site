Some nights are quiet…but some little hearts feel full.

Full of thoughts.

Full of feelings.

Full of things they don’t yet know how to say.

This is a gentle bedtime story for those moments—for children learning to understand their big feelings 🤍 

---



High up in the soft blue sky lived a little cloud.

She was small, fluffy, and kind…but she had a habit.

Whenever she felt something—a little sadness, a little worry, even a tiny fear—she didn’t let it out.

She kept it all inside.

One day, the wind blew a little too strongly.

The little cloud felt scared.

But she said nothing.

Another day, she saw other clouds floating freely, laughing and dancing in the sky.

She felt left out.

But she said nothing.

Drop by drop… feeling by feeling…she held everything inside.

Slowly, the little cloud began to change.

She wasn’t light and bouncy anymore.

She felt heavy… tired… quiet.

“*I don’t understand*”, she whispered. “*Why do I feel like this?*”

She tried to float higher…but she couldn’t.

Just then, a soft, gentle breeze came by.

“*Little cloud*”, it asked kindly, “*why are you holding so much inside?*”

The cloud replied softly, “*I don’t want to cry… I don’t want to let go.*”

The breeze smiled gently.

“**But holding everything inside is what makes you heavy.**”

The cloud stayed quiet for a moment.

Then slowly… *very* slowly…she allowed one tiny drop to fall.

Then another.

And another.

Soon, soft rain began to fall from her.

Not loud. Not scary.

Just gentle… and calm.

As the rain fell, something beautiful happened.

The little cloud began to feel lighter.

Her heaviness slowly melted away.

She could float again.

She could breathe again.

And as the sun peeked through, a soft rainbow appeared nearby.

The cloud smiled.

---



**🤍 A Gentle Lesson**

That night, the sky felt peaceful again.

And the little cloud understood something important:

✨ It’s okay to let your feelings out.

✨ It’s okay to cry, to talk, to release.

✨ That’s what makes your heart light again.

## *🌙 A Gentle Goodnight Thought*

Dear little one,

Just like the cloud…you don’t have to hold everything inside.

Your feelings are safe.

Your tears are okay.

Your heart is learning and growing every day 🤍

### *For Parents:*

If your child is experiencing big feelings, this story can help them understand that:

-It’s okay to feel overwhelmed

-It’s okay to express emotions

-Letting go brings calm and relief

**You can gently ask:**

“*Is there anything your little cloud wants to let go today?*”

✨ Save this story for nights when your child needs a little extra comfort 🌙
